Open projects

Do you wish to write a Bachelor's or Master's thesis in chronobiology, vision science/visual neuroscience, or computational modelling, or complete an internship?

We consider applications to join our teams in Munich and Tübingen on a rolling basis. We offer a supportive environment.

Please send your application and inquiries to Prof. Dr. Manuel Spitschan with the following documents:

  • Cover letter including dates of the envisaged project
  • CV (max. 2 pages)
