External funding

We are happy to discuss applications for external funding.

Applicants from abroad should find opportunities on Research in Germany , the pages of the Deutscher Akademischer Austauschdienst (DAAD) and the Alexander von Humboldt Foundation . There may also be other opportunities from your home country.

Please send your inquiry to Prof. Dr. Manuel Spitschan with the following documents:

  • Cover letter including dates of the envisaged project
  • CV (max. 2 pages)
  • Information about the identified funding mechanism
