Research reporting tool

ENLIGHT Checklist Wizard

Complete the ENLIGHT reporting checklist for laboratory studies of the non-visual effects of light in humans. The wizard guides you through the checklist, lets you save and reopen a draft, and fills the official checklist PDF with your answers. Guidance and the Explanation & Elaboration document are available alongside the form.

Web application

luox

luox is a free-to-use and open-source tool for calculating light- and lighting-related quantities directly in the browser. The tool was inspired by the development of minimum reporting guidelines for reporting the stimulus conditions involving light as an intervention in chronobiology, sleep research and environmental psychology experiments.

R package

LightLogR

LightLogR is an R package that facilitates working with light logger data and is dedicated to FAIR principles. LightLogR implements functions for easy import from a range of devices and provides functions for validation, analysis, and visualization. It is part of the MeLiDos project, which aims to create an ecosystem of software tools designed for working with light data from wearable devices.
