Choosing a wearable light logger means looking beyond a device's specifications. Which measurements have been tested, under what conditions, and against which reference?

Our new [Wearable light logger inventory](https://resources.tscnlab.org/lightloggers/) brings published validation evidence together in an interactive directory. Researchers can browse devices and studies, explore evidence on photopic illuminance, melanopic equivalent daylight illuminance (EDI) and angle dependence, and follow links to the original papers.

The inventory makes it easier to find out what has been examined and where evidence is still missing. Study summaries describe the tests and their findings, while the downloadable CSV supports further exploration of the catalogue.

This is a work in progress. We welcome missing studies, corrections and suggestions through the site's submission form or by [email to Manuel Spitschan](mailto:manuel.spitschan@tum.de).
