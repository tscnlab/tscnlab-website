Good reporting guidance is easier to use when the practical steps are straightforward. The new [ENLIGHT Checklist Wizard](https://resources.tscnlab.org/enlight/) helps researchers complete the reporting checklist for laboratory studies of the non-visual effects of light in humans.

The wizard guides you through the reporting items, provides explanations, and lets you record where the relevant information appears in your manuscript. You can save and reopen a draft, then download the official checklist PDF filled with your responses.

ENLIGHT was developed with international colleagues and [published in eBioMedicine in 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2023.104889). The wizard puts that existing guidance into an interactive workflow. The checklist supports transparent reporting; it does not provide a study-quality score.

The [ENLIGHT website](https://enlight-statement.org/) provides the checklist and its Explanation & Elaboration document. Please cite the original paper when using the checklist.

[Try the wizard](https://resources.tscnlab.org/enlight/) and read [Manuel Spitschan's announcement on LinkedIn]({{ page.linkedin_url }}). Feedback and suggestions are welcome.
