<p>We recently wrapped up a 3-day lab retreat, fostering a collaborative, inclusive and inventive environment. Bringing together twelve full-time researchers from the MPI for Biological Cybernetics in Tübingen and from the Professorship Chronobiology & Health at TUM, we converged for workshops, brainstorming sessions, and team-building activities, revitalizing our spirits in Castle Schreckenstein near Lake Constance. Empowered with fresh ideas and strengthened bonds, the team returns with a rekindled passion for cutting-edge research and a unified drive to conquer new scientific frontiers.</p>
