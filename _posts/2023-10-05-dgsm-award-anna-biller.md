<p>It’s our great pleasure to announce that Dr. Anna M. Biller , has been awarded research funding from the German Sleep Society (DGSM) . Anna received research funding of approx. €10,000 for her research project “EcoSleep”, a project on the long-term recording of sleep architecture and determinants of sleep architecture. This ensures a timely start of the project. More details on the project coming soon!</p>

<p>Congratulations, Anna!</p>
