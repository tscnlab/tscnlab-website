<p>From 14 to 16 April 2024, Prof. Manuel Spitschan chaired the Ladenburg Roundtable on the impact of light on human health and well-being, supported by by the Daimler and Benz Foundation. In attendance were 20 international scientists, including team members Dr Maydel Fernandez-Alonso and Dr Johannes Zauner. One output of the Roundtable is a white paper on communicating the evidence.</p>
