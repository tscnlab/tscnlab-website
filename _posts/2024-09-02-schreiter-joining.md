<p>We’re thrilled to welcome Marie Luise Schreiter to our team at the Max Planck Institute for Biological Cybernetics.</p>

<p>Joining us from the University of Tübingen, Marie Luise has a strong background in experimental psychology and neuroscience, focusing on understanding how our brain shapes the perception of the world. After completing her BSc and MSc at the University of Sussex in the UK, she completed her PhD at the Technical University of Dresden in 2020. Beyond her scientific research, she has a passion for communicating science to a variety of stakeholders.</p>

<p>At the MPI, Marie Luise will be leading a new science communication roadshow planned for 2025. The goal will be to bridge science and society, and bring topics surrounding our research areas to be meaningful to everyone.</p>

<p>Welcome to the team, Marie Luise!</p>
