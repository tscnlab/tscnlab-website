<p>We are pleased to announce that Dr. Johannes Zauner, a member of our team, has been awarded the “ LiTG-Nachwuchspreis Licht | Technik | Gestaltung ” 2023 by the German Lighting Technology Society (LiTG). This award recognizes his doctoral dissertation, “ Ocular light effects on human autonomous function: the role of intrinsically photosensitive retinal ganglion cell sensitivity and time of day ”, completed through a cooperation of the Ludwig-Maximilian University of Munich and Munich University of Applied Sciences.</p>

<p>Dr. Zauner’s work, which contributes to our understanding on the effects of light on human physiology, exemplifies the high standard of research pursued in our lab. We congratulate Dr. Zauner on this achievement and are delighted to have him as part of our team​​​​.</p>
