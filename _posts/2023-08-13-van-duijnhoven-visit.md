<p>In July 2023, Dr. Juliëtte van Duijnhoven , Assistant Professor in the Building Lighting group within the Department of the Built Environment at Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e), visited our groups at TUM in Munich and at the MPI in Tübingen. The focus of her visit was to exchange knowledge, develop joint research proposals and build collaborative potential. The visit was done as part of and through the support of the EuroTech Universities Alliance .</p>

<p>As part of her visit, she delivered a lecture as part of the EuroTeQ – Engineering University Summer School “Measuring and characterising light and lighting for human health” entitled “Personal lighting conditions: application in the built environment”.</p>
