<p>The group is launching a new podcast – Light O’Clock ! Hosted by Carolina Guidolin, the podcast delves into the impact of light on our circadian clock. A range of experts in the field explain how we – humans – process light, how it can affect our health, and share their insight and advice on light exposure.</p>

<p>The guest for the very first episode is the groups’ PI Manuel Spitschan, Research Group Leader at the Max Planck Institute for Biological Cybernetics of Tübingen & Rudolf Mößbauer Tenure Track Assistant Professor at the Technical University of Munich. The 20-minute discussion dives into biological circadian rhythms and why we should take into account the significant importance of correct light exposure on our daily lives.</p>

<p>New episodes come out every other Tuesday. You don’t want to miss out – you can listen to the new podcast on Spotify , Apple Podcasts and Podcast.de !</p>
