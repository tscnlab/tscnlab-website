<p>Every year, the lab welcomes summer interns from around the world to work with us, usually through structured programmes. This year, we have a total of four interns joining.</p>

<p>Through the CaCTüS programme at MPI:</p>

<p>Erwin Obias will be joining to work on spectral calibration of cameras across different cameras, working with Niloufar Tabandeh on her SCENES dataset.</p>

<p>Bruno Bustos will be working with Maydel Fernandez-Alonso on dynamic accommodation across wavelengths.</p>

<p>Through the TUM PREP programme at TUM:</p>

<p>Mithish Ravisankar Geetha and Tre Ridgway-Davis will be joining the team to scope out and develop prototypes for versioning in our momenTUM EMA application, working with Natalia Petliak, Anna Biller and Manuel Spitschan.</p>
