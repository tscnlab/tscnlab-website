<p>We received funding from the Bavarian Research Alliance to support a research exchange with the National Research Council in Canada. The award, which will support Niloufar Tabandeh (PhD student), to visit the research group of Dr. Jennifer Veitch in Ottawa. Jennifer has been Niloufar’s mentor within her PhD programme, and the support will strengthen our collaboration.</p>

<p>In the project, we will examine the spatial and temporal properties of environmental lighting through a novel, multi-modal approach. We’re looking forward to a productive 2023!</p>
