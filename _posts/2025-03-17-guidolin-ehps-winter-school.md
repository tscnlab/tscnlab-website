<p>On 11 and 12 March, 2025, Carolina Guidolin attended the Winter School on multi-level modelling , organized by the Synergy Network of the European Health Psychology Society in Berlin, Germany. This intensive two-day workshop provided valuable insights into using mixed-level models to analyse repeated measures data – an approach frequently used in chronobiology and other research fields involving human participants.</p>

<p>The workshop combined theoretical discussions with hands-on practice in R, allowing attendees to apply multi-level modelling techniques to real-world datasets. Beyond the technical learning, it was a great opportunity to exchange ideas with researchers from various disciplines in health psychology.</p>

<p>A huge thanks to the organizers for an engaging and informative event – and for making everyone feel welcome with a city tour!</p>
