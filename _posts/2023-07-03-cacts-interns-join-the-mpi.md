<p>We are happy to welcome Girma Sisay Negashe, who is joining us as a summer intern as part of the CaCTüS programme (Computation and Cognition Tübingen Summer Internship) at the Max Planck Institute for Biological Cybernetics . The CaCTüS Internship program is designed to support talented students facing significant challenges in pursuing higher education and scientific development.</p>

<p>Girma, a skilled researcher with expertise in machine learning, image processing, eHealth, computer vision, and data science. He's been actively involved in software development since 2019, designing problem-solving systems. Girma is also an accomplished educator, teaching programming, database, data structure, and related subjects to university students at the University of Gondar. As a dedicated researcher, Girma has made significant contributions to eHealth, collaborating with health professionals and engaging in various research projects with the eHealth Lab Ethiopia research group .</p>

<p>In our group, Girma will be focusing on the analysis of natural scenes under the guidance of Prof. Dr. Manuel Spitschan and Niloufar Tabandeh.</p>
