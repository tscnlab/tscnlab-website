Collaborations

We lead a module in the Integrative Human Circadian Daylight Platform, collaborating closely with Prof. Christian Cajochen, Dr. Mirjam Münch and Dr. Corrado Garbazza.

Through the NetIAS Constructive Advanced Thinking programme, we collaborated with an international team on “Light as a Key Predictor of Human Health and Well-being” (2022–2024). The collaboration contributed to the ENLIGHT reporting guidelines.

As part of Laura Hainke’s PhD, we are collaborating with Prof. Josef Priller (TUM), Prof. Markus Ploner (TUM) and Dr. James Dowsett (LMU).

If you’re interested in collaborating, please reach out to Prof. Dr. Manuel Spitschan .
